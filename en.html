How to earn on TikTok

TikTok monetization includes several income sources: video rewards, audience support, sales and brand partnerships. Below are the main creator tools and their differences. Access to each must be checked separately in TikTok Studio.

01Creator Rewards Program

A rewards program for original videos of at least one minute. Eligible videos begin earning after 1,000 qualified views. The dashboard shows standard and additional rewards. Amounts depend on content performance: total plays do not equal payable views. Creators track analytics, estimates and individual video results in TikTok Studio.

Learn more from TikTok

02TikTok LIVE and gifts

Viewers can support creators with virtual gifts during live streams. TikTok uses Diamonds and other factors to determine LIVE rewards under its terms. This format suits conversations, broadcasts and regular audience meetups. A gift’s purchase price is not the creator’s payout; LIVE access and rewards eligibility are checked separately.

Learn more from TikTok

03Video Gifts

Gifts may also be available on regular videos, letting audiences support posts they enjoy. This tool has its own requirements for account age, followers, activity and standing. Check Video Gifts in TikTok Studio. CRP activation does not mean video gifts are enabled.

Learn more from TikTok

04Subscription

Paid subscriptions create a closer connection with engaged viewers through exclusive videos, streams, posts and bonuses. Income comes from paying subscribers, not just public reach. Creators should plan regular content that gives subscribers a reason to return each month.

Learn more from TikTok

05TikTok Shop and Affiliate

Creators can recommend products in videos and streams and earn commission on eligible sales through TikTok Shop Affiliate. Sellers use Shop for their products. Audience trust, recommendation quality and actual purchases matter. Commission, products and partnership terms depend on the offer.

Learn more from TikTok

06Brands, TikTok One and UGC

Brand integrations are paid by agreement. TikTok One helps brands and creators collaborate. UGC is another option: making videos for a brand’s own use. Agree on content scope, timing, usage rights and payment before starting. Commercial posts require the appropriate disclosure.

Learn more from TikTok

07TikTok Series

Series groups videos into paid collections, such as lessons, detailed guides or themed series. Viewers pay for access, and creators explain what is included beforehand. Series has its own eligibility and payment terms, which must be checked separately from CRP.

Learn more from TikTok

08Effect House and artist campaigns

Interactive-effect creators may join Effect House rewards programs and challenges or work with brands. Work with Artists music campaigns are another option when available in Studio. Payment depends on each campaign’s terms; there is no fixed rate for using any effect or track.

Effect House programs

09TikTok GO

Content about hotels, attractions and local services can link to bookings through TikTok GO. Creators may earn commissions and campaign payments. This suits travel and place reviews, where viewers open offers from posts. Check partners, payable actions and eligibility within the program.

Learn more from TikTok

10TikTok Pulse and Pulse Premiere

Pulse advertising formats offer separate revenue-sharing models for eligible creators and publishers. Pulse Premiere targets partner media and premium content. These are specialist advertising programs with their own selection criteria, not an automatic CRP bonus. Confirm access and terms separately.

Learn more from TikTok

11Your own products and services

TikTok can bring buyers to consultations, courses, digital products and physical goods. Show the problem your offer solves, then guide interested viewers to purchase. Income depends on demand and sales of your own product; CRP activation is not inherently required.

12Affiliate links and direct orders

External affiliate programs pay for buyer actions under the seller’s terms, such as a paid order you referred. Content can also sell creative services, filming and editing. Disclose commercial recommendations and understand reward terms in advance. These are separate agreements, not TikTok view rewards.
