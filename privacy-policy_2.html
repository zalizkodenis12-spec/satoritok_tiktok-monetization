Privacy policy

1. Service and contact

This policy explains how personal data is handled on the website of SATORITOK and when you contact us about accounts, consultations, the community and related assistance.

For questions about data processing, access, correction or deletion, contact the service representative: @satori_tok on Telegram. Obtain and agree on the provider’s full legal name and details when arranging your order, before paying.

This is an independent service. TikTok, PayPal, Payoneer, Telegram and other external platforms have their own terms and policies.

2. Data we use

Interactive examples. Region, follower count, consultation duration and calculator options remain in the open page’s memory. They are not sent to an order database and reset on reload. Phone screens contain fictional figures and masked details.

Contact form. Submitting the form sends your name, chosen messenger, WhatsApp number or Telegram username and requested service to the website server, then to our representative via Telegram. WhatsApp is your chosen reply channel; we do not send WhatsApp messages on your behalf. Fields remain in page memory until submission. Confirmation appears after the delivery service responds; errors allow a retry or direct contact. This form does not request passwords or documents.

Language and theme. Language and theme preferences are saved for browsing convenience. On the first visit, the site uses your browser language and device theme. Manual choices are stored in localStorage until you clear browser data and in a cookie for up to one year. A language-entry marker is kept in sessionStorage until the tab closes. You can change these preferences in the site header.

Website visits. The hosting provider may process IP addresses, browser information, request times and technical logs to deliver pages, control access and maintain security. Google Ads loads only after consent to advertising cookies and is used to measure advertising performance. Ad personalization is disabled. Your choice is stored in localStorage for up to 180 days and can be changed using Cookie settings at the bottom of the page. After withdrawal, new advertising cookies are not permitted; previously stored cookies can be deleted in your browser settings. This website has no payment-details collection forms. Hosting may use necessary session and authentication mechanisms.

Enquiries and orders. On Telegram we receive the messages you share voluntarily, your name or alias, contact, order preferences and information needed to complete the order. Depending on the service, this may include an account ID, problem description, agreed terms and payment confirmation.

Documents and access. The website does not accept passwords, identity documents, bank card numbers or tax IDs. If agreed assistance requires account access, we discuss the access process and necessary information separately. Only accurate information and the customer’s own documents are used. Do not include sensitive details in public messages.

3. Why we process data

We use data to answer enquiries, agree and complete orders, grant access, handle warranty claims, prevent abuse and meet mandatory record-keeping requirements.

Where a legal basis is required, processing relies on requested pre-contract steps or contract performance, legal obligations, or legitimate interests in security and dispute protection. Separate consent is requested where needed. Consent may be withdrawn for future processing without affecting earlier lawful processing.

Visiting the website does not consent to marketing messages. Demo selections are not used for automated decisions with legally significant effects.

4. External services and transfers

Order buttons open Telegram. Some include your selected options in a draft-message link, which Telegram receives when opened; you send the message yourself. The separate contact form sends the entered details to our server when you press Send and forwards the request to our representative on Telegram without requiring you to open the app.

The header logo opens the home page; the footer brand links to TikTok and reviews link to Telegram. External platforms have their own privacy policies. PayPay, Payopeer and bank-transfer demos do not connect payment systems or carry out transactions.

Information needed for an agreed order may be processed by the hosting provider, messaging platform, TikTok or selected payment provider within their roles. We do not sell personal data. Disclosure may also be necessary to comply with law or protect legal claims.

External providers may be in other countries. International transfers are subject to legally required conditions and safeguards; specific mechanisms depend on the provider and applicable law.

5. Retention and security

Order information is kept as long as needed for completion, support, claims and mandatory retention periods. Dispute records may be held until resolution. Data no longer needed for a valid purpose is deleted or anonymized.

Data access should be limited to people needed to complete the order. After assistance, end any access sessions you granted, check linked services and update account security. Do not share documents or details unnecessary for the agreed task.

Hosting logs and external-platform data are also subject to those providers’ retention policies. No transmission or storage method can guarantee absolute security.

6. Requests and rights

Within applicable law, you may request processing information, access and a copy, correction, deletion, restriction or portability, and object on legally permitted grounds. A proportionate identity check may be needed to respond.

Send your request through @satori_tok on Telegram, indicating the contact used for your order and what you need. Do not attach a full identity document unnecessarily. Deletion may not be immediate where records must legally be retained. We respond within the period required by applicable law.

If EU data-protection rules apply, you may contact the competent supervisory authority. Learn about your rights: official Your Europe portal.
