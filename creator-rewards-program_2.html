TikTok monetization: how Creator Rewards Program works

VLADMONETIZATION · 6 September 2026

Active monetization is only one part of being a creator. Before planning your posts, understand which features are connected, where to find results and what remains your responsibility.

What is Creator Rewards Program?

Creator Rewards Program, or CRP, rewards creators of eligible original TikTok videos. Official terms specify at least 10,000 followers, 100,000 views in the last 30 days and videos of one minute or longer. These are only part of the requirements: availability, age and account standing also matter.

Source: Creator Rewards Program terms in TikTok Help.

What to check in a ready account

Start with three separate questions: can you log in, is the rewards section available, and are payouts configured? A screenshot of one screen does not answer the other questions.

Our account builder lets you choose a region and follower count. Confirm the specific profile and included access before payment. Keep the agreed terms in your conversation to compare the delivered account with your order.

  • Region and follower count match your order.
  • You can access the profile and the email included in the order.
  • You know where to check monetization status.
  • Preparation time, support and warranty are agreed.

Getting started after delivery

Choose a topic you can regularly create your own material about. Prepare several ideas, record your footage and keep the editing project. This helps you revisit effective techniques and explain your content’s origins.

For your first series, keep a simple log: topic, publication date, length, views, audience response and rewards. Compare equal time windows so a single good or bad day does not drive your conclusions.
