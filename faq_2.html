CRP is for creators with a personal account and accurate information. The standard minimum age is 18; some terms require 19. Business profiles are not eligible. TikTok may request identity verification.

The numerical application requirements are at least 10,000 followers and 100,000 views in the last 30 days. Reaching these figures does not replace other criteria or TikTok’s review. An already connected account’s current participation status must be checked separately.

An eligible video must be at least one minute long. Short videos can grow your audience but do not themselves earn CRP rewards. Add length through useful explanations, stories or your own analysis, rather than empty repetition.

The program is designed for content you conceived, filmed and created yourself. Reuploads, another creator’s watermark, minimal edits to others’ work, Duet, Stitch, slideshows and repetitive loops do not meet this criterion. Video and audio rights also matter.

CRP counts eligible videos posted after joining the program. Previously published videos do not earn rewards retroactively. Plan new original posts after activation is confirmed.

These are unique eligible views from the For You feed. Artificial, paid and promoted views, views under five seconds and other ineligible views are excluded under TikTok’s rules. The public counter and rewarded view count can therefore differ.

An eligible video starts accumulating rewards after 1,000 qualified views. This is a per-video threshold. The 100,000 views in 30 days is a separate program application criterion.

Advertising, paid integrations and sponsored content do not qualify for CRP rewards. Brand partnerships can provide separate income. Keep these sources distinct and use the required commercial-content disclosure.

Platform violations, inaccurate information, artificial engagement and manipulation can disqualify content or accounts. Check Studio notifications. If you believe a decision is wrong, use the in-app appeal process and follow its deadlines.

Standard rewards depend on qualified views and RPM — rewards per thousand such views. Additional rewards consider quality, engagement and content specialization. TikTok does not guarantee a single amount per million views. Our home-page calculator is a separate illustrative model.
