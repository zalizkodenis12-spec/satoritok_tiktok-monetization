1. Before placing an order

SATORITOK offers digital accounts and creator services. Orders and terms are arranged through @satori_tok on Telegram. This website helps you explore options and services; it does not accept online payments.

Before paying, agree on the account or service, price and currency, items included, delivery time, payment method, provider details and applicable terms. Account prices depend on your options and are confirmed individually. Selecting options does not reserve an account or place a paid order.

Phone interfaces and calculator figures are demonstrations. They do not verify the income or condition of any offer. Check the purchased profile’s CRP status, access and history before paying.

Information to obtain before buying: contract information on Your Europe.

2. Digital delivery and completion

No physical shipping is needed. Accounts are ready: after purchase we prepare the profile for handover, so you do not wait for account creation or audience growth. Standard delivery takes 12–24 hours after purchase at no extra charge. Urgent preparation takes 3–5 hours and costs an extra 20% of the profile price. For a €265 profile, the surcharge is €53, making €318 in total. Timing and the final amount are agreed before payment. Other services have separately agreed delivery times.

  • Account: we transfer the agreed login details and linked access included in your order. You check the login, profile details and program status.
  • Identity or tax information assistance: we provide the agreed assistance using accurate customer information. TikTok and payment providers make their own decisions.
  • CRP activation assistance: we first check eligibility, then provide the agreed application assistance. Approval depends on TikTok’s requirements and decision.
  • Consultation: held at an agreed time, in your chosen 30- or 60-minute format.
  • Private community: permanent access to the community and included materials for an agreed one-time payment.

Completion and the items delivered are recorded in our messages. If anything agreed is missing or does not work, contact us through your order conversation.

3. 30-day warranty

The commercial warranty lasts 30 calendar days from the account purchase. It covers account bans not caused by the buyer. A non-working profile is eligible for replacement after we review the claim.

We review TikTok notices, account history and use after handover to determine the cause of a ban. Replacement details are agreed with the buyer for a confirmed warranty claim.

If the buyer caused the ban through prohibited content, artificial engagement, false information or other platform violations, compensation or replacement under this commercial warranty is not provided.

The warranty does not promise any reach, RPM or income from future posts. It supplements, rather than replaces, mandatory buyer rights under applicable law.

4. Returns after completion

We do not offer voluntary refunds for completed orders. Our commercial terms provide a replacement for a non-working profile when the warranty conditions are met.

Completion means delivering what was agreed: providing access, completing the assistance, holding the consultation or granting community access. Payment alone does not make an unfinished order complete.

This policy does not override refunds, price reductions, remedies for defects or other protections required by applicable law. Mandatory rights take priority over our voluntary policy.

EU rights concerning defective digital products: digital contracts — European Commission.

5. Cancellation and rescheduling

If the order is not yet complete and you want to cancel, contact us through @satori_tok on Telegram as soon as possible. Cancellation, rescheduling and the final settlement depend on agreed terms, work already done and mandatory law. Consultation rescheduling terms are agreed when booking.

Any statutory withdrawal period for distance contracts remains applicable. Visiting this website or opening Telegram does not waive that right.

Where starting a service or supplying digital content before the withdrawal period ends requires express consent, acknowledgement of the consequences and contractual confirmation, these must be obtained beforehand. Rules may differ for fully performed services and digital content.

Learn more: European Commission guidance on consumer rights. Applicability depends on the parties and circumstances of the order.

6. How to request a replacement

Contact us through @satori_tok on Telegram using the contact you placed the order with. Include the purchase date, profile ID, problem description and a ban notice or other error.

Hide unnecessary personal and payment details in screenshots. Do not send a full passport, password or tax ID with your initial request. Any further information needed will be discussed separately.

After reviewing the cause and purchase date, we will explain whether the warranty applies and agree on next steps. Keep the conversation and order confirmations until the issue is resolved.

A short interactive example is available in the FAQ section. It does not replace a review of your individual claim.

7. Data and mandatory rights

Our handling of request information is described in the privacy policy. Provider details, applicable law and terms are confirmed before payment.

These terms apply only to the extent permitted by mandatory provisions of applicable law. Mandatory law takes priority in case of conflict.
